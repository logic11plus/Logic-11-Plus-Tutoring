Pedagogy

Our Approach to 11+ Maths

Grammar and independent school 11+ exams reward adaptability and structured reasoning. Here is how we develop independent, resilient mathematical thinkers.

01

First-Principles Understanding

Rather than relying on mechanical rote learning, we explore why mathematical rules work. When children understand the fundamental structure of a concept, unfamiliar and non-routine exam questions become approachable.

02

Multi-Step Problem Dissection

Complex 11+ maths questions require breaking problems into logical stages. We teach structured problem-solving heuristics: identifying core constraints, sketching visual models, and systematically reasoning backwards.

03

Mathematical Dialogue

In our small online groups, students explain their thinking aloud and compare alternative methods. Articulating a mathematical strategy sharpens reasoning and reinforces deep long-term retention.

04

Constructive Error Analysis

We treat mistakes as essential learning milestones. Students learn to systematically classify errors—distinguishing between conceptual gaps and computational slips—to build self-correcting precision.
