Our Philosophy

Our Core Approach to 11+ Maths

Grammar and independent school entrance exams assess adaptability, structured thinking, and multi-step reasoning. Here is how we build genuine mathematical mastery.

01
💡

First-Principles Understanding

We go far beyond memorising mechanical shortcuts. By uncovering why mathematical principles work, children develop intuitive conceptual schemas that allow them to unpack novel, unseen questions with ease.

02
🧩

Multi-Step Problem Dissection

Challenging 11+ questions often conceal multiple logical layers. We equip students with structured heuristics: identifying variables, sketching visual diagrams, and systematically working backwards.

03
💬

Collaborative Dialogue

In our small online groups, students articulate their reasoning and compare multiple solution paths. Explaining a method solidifies comprehension and builds articulate, resilient thinkers.

04
🎯

Constructive Error Analysis

Mistakes are valuable learning moments. We guide students to classify their errors—differentiating conceptual gaps from calculation slips—fostering independent self-correction.

Structured Syllabus

Comprehensive 11+ Mathematics Syllabus

Meticulously structured for GL Assessment, CEM, and Independent School entrance examinations.

Module 1

Number & Arithmetic Mastery

  • Fractions, Decimals & Percentages
  • Ratios, Proportions & Unitary Methods
  • Factors, Multiples, Primes & Indices
  • Negative Numbers & Order of Operations (BIDMAS)
Module 2

Algebraic Thinking & Patterns

  • Formulating Expressions & Solving Equations
  • Number Sequences & Nth Term Patterns
  • Function Machines & Algebraic Substitution
  • Worded Problem Translation
Module 3

Geometry, Shape & Space

  • Perimeter, Area & Composite Shapes
  • Volume, Surface Area & 3D Nets
  • Angle Properties & Polygons
  • Transformations, Coordinates & Reflections
Module 4

Applied Logic & Problem Solving

  • Averages (Mean, Median, Mode) & Range
  • Venn Diagrams & Carroll Diagrams
  • Probability & Combinatorics
  • Multi-Step Non-Routine Problems
