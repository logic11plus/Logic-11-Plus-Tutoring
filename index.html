Pedagogy & Philosophy

Our Core Approach to 11+ Maths

Mathematics at the 11+ level demands reasoning, adaptability, and the ability to dissect complex questions. Here is how we develop true mathematical fluency.

01
🧠

First-Principles Understanding

Instead of memorising mechanical formulas, students uncover why mathematical methods work. We deconstruct topics to fundamental concepts so students can tackle novel, unseen questions with ease.

02
🧩

Multi-Step Problem Solving

Modern 11+ grammar and independent school exams reward deep logical reasoning. We teach structured problem-solving heuristics: identifying variables, sketching representations, and systematically working backwards.

03
💬

Socratic Group Dialogue

In our small online groups, students verbalise their reasoning and compare multiple solution paths. Explaining a method solidifies retention and reveals alternative, more elegant strategies.

04
🔍

Error Analysis & Metacognition

Mistakes are high-value teaching moments. We systematically categorise errors (conceptual, misread questions, computational slips) so students gain self-correcting awareness and precision.
