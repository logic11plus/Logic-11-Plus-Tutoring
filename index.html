Simultaneous Equations
3 pens + 2 rulers = £4.80
1 pen + 2 rulers = £2.40
↓ subtract equation (2) from (1)
2 pens = £2.40 ⇒ 1 pen = £1.20
£1.20 + 2 rulers = £2.40 ⇒ 2 rulers = £1.20
⇒ 1 ruler = 60p ✓
Fraction Division (Keep-Flip-Multiply)
Problem: 3 1/2 ÷ 7/12
= 7/2 × 12/7
= (7 × 12) / (2 × 7) = 84 / 14
= 6 ✓
Check: 6 × 7/12 = 42/12 = 3 1/2 ✓
Speed, Distance & Time
Depart: 08:35 | Arrive: 09:20
Time = 45 mins = 3/4 hour = 0.75 hr
Distance = 48 miles
Speed = Distance ÷ Time
= 48 ÷ 0.75 = 48 × (4/3)
= 16 × 4 = 64 mph ✓
Ratio Sharing (Baking Recipe)
Flour : Sugar : Butter = 5 : 3 : 2
Total parts = 5 + 3 + 2 = 10 parts
Total mass = 600g
1 part = 600g ÷ 10 = 60g
Flour = 5 × 60g = 300g
Sugar = 3 × 60g = 180g, Butter = 120g ✓
Consecutive Numbers Algebra
3 consecutive odd numbers add up to 99
Let numbers be: n, n+2, n+4
n + (n+2) + (n+4) = 99
3n + 6 = 99 ⇒ 3n = 93 ⇒ n = 31
Numbers: 31, 33, 35 ✓
Check: 31 + 33 + 35 = 99 ✓
Reverse Percentages (Sale)
Coat reduced by 25% now costs £135
Original price = 100%
Sale price = 100% - 25% = 75% = £135
1% = £135 ÷ 75 = £1.80
100% = £1.80 × 100 = £180.00 ✓
Compound L-Shape Geometry
Split into Rect A (8cm × 4cm) & Rect B (6cm × 3cm)
Area A = 8 × 4 = 32 cm²
Area B = 6 × 3 = 18 cm²
Total Area = 32 + 18 = 50 cm² ✓
Perimeter = 8 + 4 + 2 + 3 + 6 + 7 = 30 cm ✓
Prime Factors, HCF & LCM
60 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5 (2² × 3 × 5)
84 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 7 (2² × 3 × 7)
Common factors = 2 × 2 × 3 = 12
HCF = 12
LCM = 12 × 5 × 7 = 420 ✓
Regular Hexagon Interior Angle
Number of sides (n) = 6
Sum of angles = (n - 2) × 180°
= (6 - 2) × 180° = 4 × 180° = 720°
Each angle = 720° ÷ 6 = 120° ✓
Exterior angle = 360° ÷ 6 = 60°
Check: 120° + 60° = 180° ✓
Fractions with Common Denominator
Calculate: 2/3 + 4/5 - 1/2
LCM of 3, 5, 2 = 30
2/3 = 20/30, 4/5 = 24/30, 1/2 = 15/30
= (20 + 24 - 15) / 30 = 29/30
Answer = 29/30 ✓
Data & Averages
Ordered set: 14, 18, 19, 21, 21, 27
Median = (19 + 21) ÷ 2 = 20
Sum = 14 + 18 + 19 + 21 + 21 + 27 = 120
Mean = 120 ÷ 6 = 20
Mode = 21, Range = 27 - 14 = 13 ✓
Arithmetic Sequence Rule
Sequence: 7, 13, 19, 25, 31...
Step difference = +6 ⇒ Start with 6n
When n = 1: 6(1) + 1 = 7
nth term formula = 6n + 1 ✓
50th term = 6(50) + 1 = 300 + 1 = 301 ✓
